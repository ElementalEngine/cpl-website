All CPL rules apply in the Premier League (see the [general rules](/rules/general/)). The Premier League Director,
Linda7, has sole authority on the rules and running of the League, with oversight from the CPL staff team.

These rules are also pinned in the CPL Discord, in #pl-news and in #pl-information-hub > PL Rules.

## Structure

### Season 6 divisions

The League has three divisions. A team's division depends on its results in the previous season. New teams start in
the lowest division, but the League Director may place a new team higher based on its players' previous League
experience, or results in CWC or in leagues of CPL's partner communities.

**Division 1:** Bams&Pochik, Change of Circumstances, Chill Bro, CivJP, Freritos, Ulyss, Unlucky, You Can't Be
Serious

**Division 2:** CivFantazery, FFA Andies, Gatix, Le Nouveau Front PoPouler, Les 4 Poneys de l'Apocalypse, Mandate
of Heaven, The Farmhouse

**Division 3:** Boomer Gaming, Crops in Disguise, Druns, Nai-Loong, Pessimistic Punjabis, Piglet Blitz, Suomi,
TT-Squad

### Season structure

A division with eight teams or fewer plays a double round robin regular season. A division with nine or more teams
plays a single round robin. The League Director may schedule extra games for a division that plays fewer games than
the others.

### Season 6 dates

Season 6 starts on 31 August 2026 at 00:00 UTC and is scheduled to end on 14 December 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Schedule
changes and deadlines for finishing games are posted in #pl-news.

### Tie breaks

If two or more teams are tied at the end of the regular season, and their place in the division matters, the tie is
broken as follows. In a tie of three or more teams, if a team drops out of the tied group at any step, start again
from step 1 with the teams left.

Two teams:

1. **Head-to-head:** the results between the tied teams. They must have played each other at least twice.
2. **Tiebreak game:** if the teams split their games, or only played each other once, they play one tiebreak game.

Three or more teams:

1. **Head-to-head sweep:** each team in the tied group must have played each of the others at least twice.
2. **Strength of victory (SOV):** see below.
3. **Tiebreak games:** a round robin between the tied teams.
4. **Slowest defeat:** if the tiebreak games are still tied.
5. **Coin flip:** if nothing else breaks the tie.

**Strength of victory:** at the end of the regular season, each team gets a base value from its final place in the
division. In an eight-team division, the last team is worth 1 point and the first team 8 points; tied teams get
fractions. A team earns points for each regular-season win, equal to the base value of the team it beat: for
example, a win over the first-place team is worth 8 points. The team with the most SOV points wins the tie break.

### Irregular seasons

- The League Director may run extra irregular seasons during CWC or CWC qualifiers, if there is enough interest.
  Teams not playing in CWC or a CWC qualifier may take part.
- Irregular season results don't affect division placement for the next regular season.
- Roster rules don't apply. Any player in good standing in CPL who isn't playing in CWC or a qualifier may play,
  including players on another team's roster.
- Players on a CWC team can't play, but may coach. Once their team is knocked out of CWC or the qualifier, they may
  play for a team in the irregular season.

### New teams

- New teams are welcome. Teams that play in other communities' leagues may also play in the Premier League, if
  their schedules let them finish games in both.
- A new team's captain may apply in the off-season, before the deadline, using the [new team
  form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1SZPYRJakpKBEaMplPkATYTQfBAdJYV37USZWhcC1A-A/edit). The deadline for Season
  6 was 17 August 2026 at 00:00 UTC.
- A team may be refused entry for disbanding too often in past seasons, for past sanctions, or for any other
  reason.

## Post-season

### Series rules

In a best-of-three (Bo3) or best-of-five (Bo5) series, no map or leader may be played more than once.

### Map draft for a series

The map draft must be finished at least 48 hours before Game 1.

For a Bo3 series:

1. The higher-seeded team bans one map.
2. The lower-seeded team bans one map.
3. For Game 1, the higher-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
4. For Game 2, the lower-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
5. The teams take turns banning one map until one map is left. The lower-seeded team makes the last ban.
6. The last map is the map for Game 3. The higher-seeded team chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.

For a Bo5 series:

1. The higher-seeded team bans one map.
2. The lower-seeded team bans one map.
3. For Game 1, the higher-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
4. For Game 2, the lower-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
5. For Game 3, the higher-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
6. For Game 4, the lower-seeded team picks the map, chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.
7. If more than one map is left, the teams take turns banning one map until one is left. The lower-seeded team
   makes the last ban.
8. The last map is the map for Game 5. The higher-seeded team chooses to be Team 1 or Team 2, and hosts.

The map draft is only finished when every step is done.

### Leader draft for a series

Use the CivDraft app for a normal leader draft in Game 1. For each later game, add every leader played in the
earlier games of the series to the auto-banned leaders. Teams may also ban leaders in each game's draft, if they
aren't already auto-banned.

### Championship

- After the regular season and any tie breaks, the 1st and 4th-place Division 1 teams play one Bo3 semifinal, and
  the 2nd and 3rd-place teams play the other. The winners play the Bo3 Championship. Each player on the winning
  team gets the "Premier League Champion" role.
- If fewer than four Division 1 teams won most of their season games, the League Director may bring in
  lower-division teams that won most of their season games, or cancel Championship games as needed.

### Advancement and relegation

- Advancement and relegation happen at the end of the regular season, including tie breaks and extra games. Every
  advancement and relegation match is a Bo5.
- The 1st-place Division 2 team is promoted to Division 1. The 8th-place Division 1 team is relegated to Division
  2.
- The 7th-place Division 1 team plays the 2nd-place Division 2 team, and the 6th-place Division 1 team plays the
  3rd-place Division 2 team. The Division 1 teams are the higher seeds. The winners play in Division 1 next season;
  the losers in Division 2.
- No extra unmuted players are allowed in Division 1/Division 2 matches.
- Advancement and relegation between Divisions 2 and 3 work the same way. One extra unmuted coach is allowed in
  these matches (see [Coaches and managers](#coaches-and-managers)).

### Coaches and managers

- Teams in Division 2 and below, and teams in every division in an irregular season, may name one coach who isn't
  on their player roster. The coach may be unmuted in the team voice channel during a game.
- Division 1 teams may name a manager who isn't on their player roster to schedule games. The manager may not join
  the team voice channel.
- A coach or manager may not play in League games unless they are moved to the player roster.

## Team rosters

### Roster size

A roster must have at least four players and at most nine, not counting a non-playing coach or manager.

### Mid-season roster changes

Season 6 rosters became official on 24 August 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Teams may make as many roster changes as they like
during the season, except that a team may not add:

- a player who has played for a team in a higher division this season
- a player it removed from its roster earlier this season, unless it uses a [bring-back token](#bring-back-tokens)

To make a change, post in #pl-team-hub > PL Roster Changes S6 in this format:

```
Team: {team name}
Departing player: {player name(s)}
Joining player: {player name(s)}
```

### One-time substitution tokens

Each team has three one-time substitution tokens for the regular season and post-season. A one-time sub must not
have played in a League game this season, or been on a League roster. To use one, post in #pl-team-hub > PL Subs S6
in this format:

```
Team: {team name}
Substitute player: {player name}
```

### Bring-back tokens

- Each team has three bring-back tokens for the regular season and post-season. A token brings back a player who
  was on the roster this season and was then removed.
- A player who was removed and then played for another team can't be brought back.
- To use a token, post in #pl-team-hub > PL Roster Changes S6, in the same format as a roster change.

### Roster change timing

Ask for roster changes and substitutions at least 24 hours before a League game, so League and team roles can be
given. Changes made later are handled on a best-effort basis. If roles haven't been given before a game, tag "@CPL
Staff".

### Off-season roster changes

Teams may make as many roster changes as they like between seasons, including adding players who played for a
higher-division team, or who were on the roster before and left.

### Team continuity

- If a team doesn't have four players who took an active part for a large part of the season, the League Director
  may review the team's ranking for post-season play or CWC qualification.
- If a team doesn't have four players who took an active part in the previous season, the League Director may
  review its division for the next regular season.

### Team name changes

A captain may ask to change the team's name at any time. The League Director must approve the new name.

## Mods

### Required mods

- Better Balanced Game (BBG) 7.5.1 [ID 2865001760]
- BBG Expanded (BBG Ex) 2.1 [ID 3533091092]
- Better Balanced Maps (BBM) 1.39.5 [ID 3179425402]
- Multiplayer Helper (MPH) 1.8.0 [ID 3307282026]
- Better Spectator Mod (BSM) 1.2.7 [ID 1916397407], for cast games: every player needs it if the game is cast

### Optional mods

- Better Report Screen [ID 1312585482]
- Extended Policy Cards [ID 2266952591]
- NotificationClear [ID 3063966792]
- Resource Visibility Sharing [ID 3139749709]
- Top Panel Extension Pro [ID 2997927787]

Most optional mods don't work for any player unless the host has them enabled.

### Disallowed mods

- Other versions of BBG, BBM, BBS or MPH must be uninstalled, not just disabled.
- Other disallowed mods, such as Sailor Cat's Configurable Goody Huts [ID 2462745561], Detailed Map Tacks [ID
  2428969051], Better Spectator Mod (if the game isn't cast) and other UI mods, must be disabled. Static mods with
  different version IDs for cloud games may stay installed, but disabled.
- Disallowed mods installed directly in the `Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\mods` folder must be
  moved out of that folder before the game starts, or the folder renamed for the game.

### Managing your mods

- To avoid installing disallowed mods by accident, set **Auto Download Additional Content** to **Disabled** in Game
  Options. If you need a mod for a casual game, subscribe to it in the Steam Workshop, then unsubscribe and restart
  Steam after the game.
- Mods downloaded automatically from a host may stay installed even if the game doesn't show them in Additional
  Content. To see the mods that are really installed, open `C:\Program Files
  (x86)\Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\289070` in Windows Explorer. Your `steamapps` folder may be somewhere
  else, depending on where Steam is installed.
- The folder lists the Steam ID of each installed mod. To remove an unknown one, open
  `https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id={mod ID}`, subscribe to the mod and restart Steam, then
  unsubscribe and restart Steam again. Check that the mod's ID is no longer in the `289070` folder.

### Mod checks

If a Premier League game is unstable, a CPL staff member may ask all players and casters to post a screenshot of
their mods. Players, casters or teams with disallowed mods installed or enabled, or without the required mods
enabled, may be penalised.

### Mod updates

If the mods are updated during the season, the League uses the latest standard version (no beta versions).

## Scheduling and starting a game

### Division schedules

- The Premier League schedules regular-season games on Challonge. Each division's Challonge schedule is linked in
  #pl-information-hub > PL Schedule Links.
- The team listed on top of a pairing is the host team, and Team 1.

### Scheduling games

- Teams should agree a game time that is reasonable for both. A time on the other team's national or religious
  holiday, in the middle of their night, or in their normal work or school hours is not reasonable.
- The team scheduler should start by offering the days and times their team can play, without waiting for the other
  team. Offer times far enough ahead for the other team to plan.
- Teams have three weeks to play each round, except in the last rounds of the season. Games from later rounds may
  only be played if neither team has a current or overdue game available.
- Every game on the schedule must be played by 14 December 2026 at 00:00 UTC. The League Director may give a
  one-week extension to a team with one unplayed game, if its date and time are announced before that deadline.
- Once a time is agreed, post the matchup, date and time, and whether the game is on live BBG or Beta, in
  #pl-team-hub > PL Scheduling. The League Director or another staff member posts the game in #pl-schedule. If it
  isn't posted there, play at the agreed time anyway.

### Postponing a game

- A scheduled game may be postponed if both teams agree.
- A game may be postponed if one team thinks the posted time isn't the one agreed, and objects within 6 hours of
  the game being posted, or 1 hour before the game, whichever is earlier.
- The League Director or other staff may postpone a game for a strong reason, such as a natural disaster near one
  of the teams, a Steam outage, or a Steam or mod update that disrupts the game.
- Once a game has started, only the League Director or other staff can postpone it. A game isn't postponed because
  one team has technical problems and the other doesn't.

### Unplayed games

- For a game more than two weeks overdue, the League Director awards a forfeit win to the team that offered the
  most reasonable available game times. The three-week deadline may be extended if both teams agree a specific date
  and time.
- If neither team offered more reasonable times, the game is marked as a tie, which counts as a loss for both
  teams. A game marked as a tie may only be played later if both teams are up to date with their schedule.

### Voice channels

- Each team has its own voice channel in the CPL Discord, grouped by division under PREMIER LEAGUE D{x} and in
  alphabetical order. Players with the "Civ Prem League" role can see every channel; only players with the team's
  role can join it.
- Every player in the game must be in their team voice channel for the whole game.
- If a player's leader dies during the game, the player must leave the voice channel or mute straight away, unless
  they are the one extra unmuted player allowed in Division 2 and below.
- Teams in Division 2 and below, and in every division in irregular seasons, may have one extra unmuted player or
  coach in the voice channel (see [Advancement and relegation](#advancement-and-relegation) for exceptions).
- Other team members may be in the voice channel to watch a player's stream and listen, but must stay muted at all
  times.
- There is no limit on team members or coaches in the voice channel during the draft.
- If the team voice channel has a problem, players may use a CPL Teamers voice channel, and post in #pl-lobby-links
  to tell their opponents where they are.

### Players in countries that ban Discord

- Discord is banned in China, Russia, Turkey, North Korea, Iran, Oman and the United Arab Emirates. Players there
  can usually reach Discord through a VPN, but can't join their team voice channel for a League game, so they don't
  have to be in it.
- A team with players who won't be in the voice channel for this reason must post their names in #pl-lobby-links
  before the game starts, and post the name of any substitute too. Players on the team who can join the voice
  channel must do so.
- Players without this exemption who aren't in the team voice channel during the game face penalties, and their
  team may be sanctioned too.

### Being on time

Teams should be in their voice channel and ready to join the lobby 10 minutes before the start time. A team that
isn't ready to start the draft at the start time is sanctioned:

- **10 minutes late:** loses its last second-rotation ban
- **15 minutes late:** loses its last first-rotation ban
- **20 minutes late:** loses its remap token
- **30 minutes late:** forfeits the game

Teams that are often late may face further sanctions.

- The host team must have a player with the CPL "Civ 6 Host" role, or let a third party, a caster or Team 2 host. A

- Post the lobby link in #pl-lobby-links at least 5 minutes before the start time. A late lobby link gets the same
  sanctions as [being late](#being-on-time).

- Players must start joining the lobby within 5 minutes of the lobby link being posted in #pl-lobby-links. If some
  players aren't there yet, their team must say in the game chat who is joining later.
- Every player's name in the game must match or be close to their CPL Discord name, and start with Roman letters.
- Don't change slots while players are joining: it can cause disconnects and desyncs once the game starts.
- Lobby order without a caster: 1-2-2-1-2-1-1-2. With one or two casters: S-S-1-2-2-1-2-1-1-2. The casters, or one
  caster and an AI, must be Spectators.
- On the Pangaea Ultima East-West map, each team's first two slots are the backline. A leader with a coastal spawn
  bias must go in a backline slot.
- All players must be in the lobby as soon as the draft ends.

## Map pool, bans and draft

### Map pool

The Season 6 regular season map pool has nine maps.

- **Core maps:** Lakes, Pangaea Classic Ridges, Rich Highlands
- **Extra maps:** Continents & Islands, Pangaea Ultima East-West (Pangaea East-West in CivDraft), Primordial, Rich
  Riverlands, Seven Seas, Tilted Axis

### Map diversity

Every Monday of the regular season except the first, the two most-played maps of the season so far are banned for
the next round, and their game counts reset to zero. They may be played again the round after. Each round's bans
are announced in #pl-information-hub > PL Regular Season Map/Leader Bans.

### Auto-banned leaders

No leaders are auto-banned in general (see [Leader diversity](#leader-diversity)). Both Malis (Mansa Musa and
Sundiata Keita) are auto-banned on the Pangaea Ultima East-West map.

### Leader diversity

Every Monday of the regular season except the first, the eight most-played leaders of the season so far are banned
for the next round, and their game counts reset to zero. They may be played again the round after. Each round's
bans are announced in #pl-information-hub > PL Regular Season Map/Leader Bans.

### CivDraft

Using [CivDraft](https://civdraft.xyz/) is encouraged but not required.

1. The host team normally creates the CivDraft lobby. Team 2 may create it if the host team asks, with the host
   team as Team 1.
2. Fill in the names of Team 1 and Team 2.
3. Use the CPL preset: map, 10 bans (5 each), 4 picks (2 each), 6 bans (3 each), 4 picks (2 each). The map pool is
   part of the preset.
4. Add every auto-banned leader from the "Autoban Civs" list.
5. Remove any banned maps from the "Maps to draft" list. CivDraft needs three maps in the pool. Set the browser to
   full screen to see the top of this box, then scroll down. Remove the Fractal map if Rich Riverlands is banned.
6. Click "Start a draft".
7. Post the link in #pl-lobby-links at least 5 minutes before the start time, or send it by DM to the other team
   and any casters. A late draft link gets the same sanctions as [being late](#being-on-time).
8. Each team member taking part in the draft clicks "Join {team name}" and changes the random name the app gives
   them to their own name. Casters click "Join Obs".
9. When everyone taking part is in the lobby, click "Ready". When both teams are ready, CivDraft starts the map
   bans.

### Bans and picks

- Any player in the draft lobby may make picks or bans. It is best for each team to choose one player to do it.
  CivDraft sometimes takes a moment to register a choice, so make your choice with a few seconds left on the timer.
- **Map bans:** Team 1 bans first, by clicking a map and then "Ban". The teams take turns, and Team 2 makes the
  last map ban. The map left is shown as the drafted map near the lower left of the leader ban screen.
- **Leader bans:** auto-banned leaders are already crossed out. The first-rotation ban order is
  1-2-1-2-1-2-1-2-1-2. Click a leader to ban it during your team's turn. The other team sees the choice straight
  away, and you may change it until you click "Ban".
- **Leader picks:** click a leader, then "Pick". The first-rotation pick order is 1-2-2-1.
- The second-rotation ban order is 2-1-2-1-2-1, and the pick order is 2-1-1-2.
- When the last pick is made, CivDraft shows every pick and ban. Click "Copy draft" to copy them. You can copy the
  draft after the game from the same CivDraft link.

### Timed-out picks and bans

- If CivDraft doesn't receive the "Pick" or "Ban" click before the timer runs out, it marks the choice as timed out
  and moves on. The team must type its pick or ban in the draft chat straight away.
- If a map ban times out, the teams must arrange the rest of the bans so that Team 2 still makes the last map ban.

### Text draft

If CivDraft isn't working, or both teams agree, the draft may be held in the game chat or a Premier League text
channel. Players are expected to keep to 60 seconds for each ban or pick.

### Final game preparation

- The host must change the map in the game settings to the drafted map by hand.
- Both teams are responsible for checking that the game settings are right.
- Players must help the other team and any spectators move into the right slots.
- Each player picks a leader from their team's draft. Players may swap slots with their teammates to arrange slot
  priority. If players keep swapping slots based on the other team's choices, Team 1 must put a player in its top
  slot, then Team 2, and so on, until the slots are set. After that, nobody may swap slots or leaders again.
- When the players are in the right slots and the settings are confirmed, each player clicks "Ready". When everyone
  is ready, MPH counts down 10 seconds and starts the game.

## In-game rules

### Sportsmanship

Players are expected to show good sportsmanship to the other team, the casters and their own teammates.

### In-game communication

- The game chat may be used between teams to wish each other "gl hf", point out stacked units or a rule break, ask
  for a Great Person to be taken or passed, report a bugged Congress, ask for a break or a relobby, and so on.
- An occasional clever remark can add to the fun, but too many can distract the other team. Insults and other
  toxicity are sanctioned.
- A team may use private chat among itself. Private messages to the other team or a caster aren't allowed.
- Players may not talk about the game with anyone who isn't in the voice channel.

### Incorrect game settings

- If players or casters notice on turn 1 that the map or another major setting is wrong, or that an observer is a
  player rather than a Spectator, the host should make a new lobby with the right settings.
- Once the game reaches turn 2, settings can't be corrected unless a team uses its remap token or there is a
  technical remap. Relobby to correct the settings, rather than using "Admin Remap".

### Technical remaps

Ask for a technical remap on or before turn 10, before any remap vote, and as soon as your team knows it has
grounds. Distances are measured from the tile where the player spawns. A technical remap doesn't use the team's
remap token.

On every map, a technical remap is granted if:

- the map has a Firaxis placement
- two players spawn 9 tiles or less from each other
- a player spawns 6 tiles or less from a city-state
- a player spawns on the edge of the map, so tiles are lost from the 1st or 2nd ring
- a player spawns without full housing
- all of a team's players spawn on the same continent
- a disaster lowers a capital's population to 1 on or before turn 10

On any Highlands, Lakes, Pangaea, Riverlands or Seven Seas map, a technical remap is also granted if a player can't
settle their 4th city (counting the capital) within 7 turns of travel from their capital (so that they settle on
the 7th turn), or before Shipbuilding.

On every other map, a technical remap is also granted if a player can't settle their 3rd city (counting the
capital) within 7 turns of travel from their capital, or before Shipbuilding.

To ask for one, tag "@CPL Staff" in #pl-lobby-links or another PL channel, so a staff member can join the team
voice channel and check. If no staff member is available, post a screenshot of the problem in #screenshots-clips so
the other team can agree to the technical remap.

### Remap tokens

1. Each team has one remap token per game.
2. If either team still has its token, the host pauses the game with a few seconds left in turn 10, opens the
   "Multiplayer Helper" (MPH) menu from the game menu, and clicks "Remap Vote". Each player votes "Yea" or "Nay"
   before the timer runs out.
3. A team without a remap token must vote "Nay". If a team splits its vote 2–2, the team votes "Yea".
4. If the vote is "Yea", don't click "Confirm" yet. Unpause with the "p" key, let the turn roll, then click
   "Confirm" in the remap window. If the window has closed, use "Admin Remap" in the MPH menu. A remap on an
   East-West map may need a relobby.
5. If the vote is "Nay", the host waits a few seconds so players can see the result, then clicks "Confirm" to close
   the vote. The map is final. Unpause with the "p" key.

If the map settings are wrong, or the game is laggy, a relobby into a new game may be used instead of "Confirm" or
"Admin Remap".

### City, religion and player names

A city may be renamed, as long as the new name doesn't:

- give away game information
- include another player's name
- use a standard city name of another civilization in the game
- break Discord's or Twitch's terms of service

Captured or absorbed city-states may not be renamed. Religion and player names follow the same rules.

### Substitutions in a game

- A team may swap a player in the game for an eligible player from its roster, or a one-time sub, for any reason
  (see [Team rosters](#team-rosters)).
- A player who was in the game or its lobby and was subbed out, or died in the game, may not sub back in.
- The sub must play the leader of the player they replace. A player staying in the game may not switch leaders.
- A player may not sub in if they have watched any part of a caster's stream or an opposing player's stream.
- If a player takes over another player's PC and plays in their place, it is a substitution. Announce it in the
  game chat and in #pl-lobby-links, and the player must change their name in the game so it is clear who is
  playing.

### Rule breaks in a game

- If a League or CPL rule is broken in a game, pause the game straight away so the teams can discuss the rule and
  how to correct it.
- The usual correction is a relobby at the start of that turn. The teams may agree on another correction in some
  cases.
- If a rule break isn't noticed on the turn it happens or the next turn, it usually can't be corrected.
- If the teams can't agree whether a rule was broken, tag "@CPL Staff" in #pl-lobby-links for a ruling.
- If staff should look at a game after it ends, upload the game logs and turn saves (see [Reporting an
  infraction](#reporting-an-infraction)).

### Conceding a game

- A team that wants to concede before either team reaches a victory condition may have one player click "Retire" in
  the game menu. The game ends and the other team gets a victory screen.
- Typing "gg" in the game chat brings up a "Are you sure you want to retire?" message. If you don't mean to end the
  game, or another player has already ended it, click "No".

## Technical problems

### Desyncs in PL games

- The game must be paused for a player who desyncs while at war.
- A desyncing player must end their turn at the same time as the slowest player who doesn't have an infinite turn.
- A player who desyncs on two turns in a row is entitled to a relobby at the end of the second turn.

### Map pins

Map pins can make the game lag a lot. Each team may pin the map, but should use pins sparingly (for example "settle
here" or "enemy settler last seen here") and delete them once they have served their purpose.

### Relobbies in PL games

- The game may relobby if both teams agree, or if a CPL staff member asks for one. A relobby can help if a player
  is desyncing or dropping, or the game is lagging. A relobby is needed to change hosts or correct an accidental
  rule break.
- It is usually best to let the current turn roll over first, so the relobby starts on a clean turn.
- The host must load the latest turn save from `Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Saves\Multi\auto`.
  If there was a rule break on the previous turn, or a problem at rollover, the teams may agree to relobby one turn
  earlier.

### Host changes

- If the game is unstable and a relobby hasn't fixed it, relobby with another player of the host team as host.
- If every player on the host team who can host has been tried, the host team may ask a spectator to host, if they
  agree. If there is no spectator, or they don't agree, relobby with a player of the other team as host.
- If that doesn't fix the problem, or causes stability or desync problems for the original host team, hosting goes
  back to the original host team.

### Mandatory substitution

After a relobby and host changes have been tried, a player must be substituted if they:

- desync every turn
- disconnect from the game five times
- have technical problems for 30 minutes in a row

If the team can't find an eligible sub, it must forfeit the game.

### Unplayable games

1. If either team thinks a game is unplayable, tag "@CPL Staff" in #pl-lobby-links. Staff will either tell the
   teams what to try, or suspend the game.
2. If no staff member is available, the teams discuss what to try, and whether the game is unplayable, together.
3. If both teams agree the game is unplayable, it may be suspended and replayed later. Post the agreement, and why,
   in #pl-team-hub > PL Scheduling or #pl-lobby-links.
4. If only one team thinks the game is unplayable, has already tried and discussed the fixes in this section with
   the other team, and no staff member is available, it types in the game chat and in #pl-lobby-links that it is
   leaving because the game is unplayable. This is not a concede.
5. Both teams must then upload `net_message_debug.log`, `net_connection_debug.log` and the turn saves (see
   [Reporting an infraction](#reporting-an-infraction)).
6. Staff review the logs and saves. If one team was mainly responsible for the instability, the League Director
   tells the more stable team to report a technical forfeit win. If neither team was mainly responsible, the game
   is suspended and replayed. If the game wasn't unreasonably unstable, or the fixes in this section weren't tried,
   the team that didn't think the game was unplayable reports a technical forfeit win.

### Suspended games

A game that can't be finished and must be replayed starts again with a new map seed on a later date. The teams may
agree to keep the same draft; if they don't agree, there is a new draft.

## Game reporting

The winning team reports the game in #pl-game-reports. The format must be exact, because a bot reads the reports to
calculate the statistics on [civfrdata.fr](https://civfrdata.fr) (CPL tab).

1. Use the CivDraft format. You can copy the draft after the game from the same CivDraft link.
2. Add a line at the top: "Div X", where X is 1, 2 or 3.
3. Replace the team names with the team roles.
4. Replace "Victory team1/team2" with the winning team's role.
5. Change "CC" to the victory type if the game ended on a victory screen.
6. Fill in the turn the game ended.
7. If the map played wasn't the drafted map, change the map.
8. If the timer ran out on any bans or picks, replace "UNKNOWN" with the ban or pick.
9. Replace "Player1" and so on with the players who played each leader.
10. Don't change the team or player order: other teams want to see how the draft was done.

The League Director reacts with the mod hammer once the result is recorded in Challonge.

## Banned exploits

An exploit is a game action the game's design didn't intend. Any new exploit that isn't on the banned or allowed
lists is not allowed. Tell the CPL staff team about any new exploit so it can be reviewed. These game actions are
not allowed:

### No city trading

Trading cities between players is not allowed.

### Liberating cities in PL games

- Bringing a dead civilization back into the game by liberating a city is not allowed. A civilization is dead as
  soon as its last city is captured, and may not be brought back during the same turn.
- Liberating a city a second time is not allowed, unless the opposing team owned the city last.
- In either case, the recaptured city must be razed.

### City-state captures and names

- Each player may capture and keep one city-state. Any other city-state the player captures must be razed.
- A player who loses their captured city-state may not capture another one.
- Capturing a city from the opposing team that used to be a city-state doesn't count towards the limit, unless it
  is captured on the same turn the opposing team captured the city-state.
- Taking over a city-state with a Great Person such as Stamford Raffles, Maria Theresa's Diplomatic Marriage
  ability, or a loyalty flip doesn't count towards the limit.

### Different moves

Players must repeat their actions if the game relobbies to the start of the turn or an earlier turn. Different
important actions, such as war moves, recruiting Great People, or chopping walls or wonders, may lead to a relobby,
an infraction or both.

### Late Great Person passing and auto-recruiting

- Once half the turn has passed, the opposing team may ask any player with the option on a Great Person to take or
  pass it. This must be the player's first action after war moves. With a quarter of the turn left, the game may be
  paused, and it only resumes once the player has announced their decision.
- A team that was forced to auto-recruit an unwanted Great Person, or couldn't recruit one because of the slot
  order, is entitled to a relobby.

### Unit stacking in PL games

- Units of the same type (land, naval, support or religious) can't be stacked on the same tile in an area of active
  combat. A tile is in an area of active combat if an enemy military unit is within 3 tiles of a player's City
  Center, Encampment or Harbor.
- As the first action of their turn, the player must unstack, make room to unstack, or shift+enter the units to
  safety. Making room may not include an attack. If the player can't unstack, they must delete units until only one
  of that type is left on the tile, before any other action.
- If an opponent attacks in the area of active combat before the player has unstacked, the player may respond to
  the war moves for a few seconds before unstacking. Responding may not include upgrading, chopping, changing
  policy cards or changing the build queue.
- If the player doesn't unstack straight after a reminder, or attacks before unstacking, the attacking player may
  pause the game and ask for the stacked unit to be deleted. If it can't be deleted because it is damaged, a
  relobby is needed.
- Civilian units, and military units outside areas of active combat, must be unstacked by the end of the player's
  turn. Great People aren't units, so this rule doesn't apply to them. Ships on land must move into water or a City
  Center as soon as possible.

### Relic trading and abuse

- Relics may not be traded before turn 20.
- Generating relics from teammates with martyr abilities is not allowed.

### Link and unlink to get around zone of control

- Using link and unlink to get around zone of control is not allowed.
- Linking a civilian or support unit to get around zone of control is allowed, if the unit isn't unlinked the same
  turn to walk several tiles through zone of control.

### Alliance duplication

Duplicating alliances by any means is not allowed.

### Resource duplication in PL games

Making trades that use a bug to create more resources than are available to trade is not allowed.

### Emergency aid and missions

Aid and emergency missions are not allowed.

### Priority targeting anti-air units

Using planes to priority target anti-air units, to kill them without taking damage, is not allowed.

### Amani abuse in PL games

If you remove Amani from a city-state and lose suzerainty over it as a result, you may not send Amani back to that
city-state for the next 5 turns.

### Victor's Embrasure promotion

Moving Victor with the Embrasure promotion out of a city and back in during the same turn, to get more than one
extra ranged shot, is not allowed.

### Pantheon abuse

- Founding more than one pantheon, or founding a pantheon more than once, is not allowed.
- Not choosing a pantheon on the turn it becomes available, to stop another player founding one the next turn, is
  not allowed.

### Duplicate religions and beliefs

- Players on the same team may not have the same beliefs.
- No two players, on the same team or not, may have religions with the same icon: it gives both players extra
  beliefs.
- Either case must be corrected straight away with a relobby.

### Phantom cities

- Settling a city in negative loyalty and then pillaging it more than once after it rebels is not allowed.
- Settling a city in negative loyalty is allowed if the city has at least four tiles that can be attacked by land
  or sea, and the team that settled it pillages it only once after it rebels.

### Trader abuse

Sending a trader to complete city-state quests, build a road, create a trading post or unlock eurekas, then
resetting the trader with the spacebar or any other way the next turn, is not allowed. Declaring war to reset the
trader after the next turn has finished is allowed.

### Negative gold per turn

- A player may not be in negative gold per turn while they have a gold-per-turn deal from another player.
- A player may send gold per turn to a teammate, as long as it doesn't put the sending player in negative gold per
  turn.
- If either happens by accident, correct it straight away with a gold-per-turn deal that takes the player out of
  negative.
- If a player is receiving gold per turn from a teammate who has died, they may not use that gold.

### Congress votes for spectators

Players may not vote for or against a spectator in the World Congress. Spectators must vote for or against one of
themselves.

### Archaeologist abuse

Trading artifacts to get more than three artifacts per Archaeologist is not allowed.

### Several unique improvements in one city

Settling a city so that it gets several unique improvements is not allowed, if the game only allows one of that
improvement per city.

### Unfinished district duplication

Using the production queue, or any other way, to get around the conditions or limits for building districts is not
allowed. For example, placing a Neighborhood, cancelling it, then placing another Neighborhood before the first is
finished.

### Sweden Nobel Peace Prize in PL games

Gaining points in a World Congress competition by trading diplomatic favour again and again is not allowed.

### Giant Death Robot corps or army in PL games

Using a Great General to form a Giant Death Robot (GDR) corps or army is not allowed.

### Third-party tools in PL games

Third-party tools are banned, including macros, scripts and auto-clickers.

### Spy abuse

Any spy created when a player loses their capital must be deleted.

### Public Transport gold

Using the Public Transport policy card to gain gold by placing Neighborhoods on farms is not allowed.

## Allowed exploits

These actions are allowed. Any exploit not listed here is banned. If you aren't sure whether a move is allowed, ask
a CPL staff member.

### Production stacking

Stacking production for one turn, by emptying the production queue and letting the turn roll over or force ending
it, is allowed.

### Shift-entering moves

Carrying out queued moves with shift+enter is allowed.

### Loyalty and settler lenses

Using the loyalty and settler lenses to estimate where the opposing team's cities and city-states are is allowed.

### Resource manipulation

Emptying a player's strategic resource reserves by queuing units and cancelling them, then accepting strategic
resources from teammates, is allowed.

### Scorched earth

Scorched earth tactics are allowed. For example, a player whose city is likely to be captured may place districts
or wonders on its tiles so the opposing team can't benefit from them.

### Attacking through units

Attacking through your own units is allowed. For example, a heavy cavalry unit may be able to attack a City Center
through a field cannon and return to its starting tile, if it has enough movement.

## Casters

### About casting

- Casters join as Spectators to give viewers a wider view of the game than a player's. Casters own their stream
  content and are free to publish it. Casting an official CPL game gives CPL permission to share the stream on
  CPL's social media.
- At most two casters may join a Premier League game as Spectators.
- A caster must be on time, so as not to delay the game, and stay for the whole game. A caster who has trouble
  continuing the cast must contact a CPL staff member.

### Caster behaviour

- Casters must uphold a good image of CPL, avoid foul language on stream, and be as unbiased as possible towards
  players and teams.
- A caster's name in the game must match or be close to their CPL Discord name.
- Harassing, insulting or disrespecting players, teams, another caster or the CPL team is not allowed. Casters are
  responsible for their co-casters' behaviour, and must moderate their stream chat to remove comments that break
  Twitch's, YouTube's or Discord's terms of service.
- Casters should ask the host to fill in the team names in the game settings, if this isn't done yet. Spectators
  then see the team names on each team's cities.
- Casters may not moderate the game or talk about it with the players, except about: wrong game settings in the
  lobby or on turn 1; an observer who is a player rather than a Spectator; or a caster's join or desync status.
- A caster who notices a rule break should tell a CPL staff member.

### Spectator votes in the World Congress

- With two spectators, they agree in private game chat that one votes A and the other votes B on each World
  Congress resolution, choosing the options least likely to affect the game.
- With one spectator, the spectator votes B on every resolution, to balance the AI spectator, which usually votes
  A, and chooses the options least likely to affect the game.

## Penalties

### Sanctions

- Infractions in Premier League games are treated more seriously than in casual games.
- An infraction by a player in a League game may lead to a CPL warning or suspension for the player, and League
  sanctions for the team.
- Team sanctions may include losing leader bans, losing remap tokens, losing a game, and disqualification from the
  season or the League.

### Reporting an infraction

Accidental rule breaks corrected in the game aren't sanctioned, unless the player or team keeps making them. Staff
decide whether a rule break was accidental from how serious it was, the circumstances and the player's history.

Staff need all the turn saves from the game and two log files. The next game overwrites these files, so save them
as soon as the game ends:

1. Paste `%LocalAppData%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Logs\` into the Windows File Explorer address
   bar.
2. Upload `net_message_debug.log` and `net_connection_debug.log`.
3. Compress the folder `Documents\My Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Saves\Multi\auto` into one file.
4. Upload all three files to the [PL infractions Google Drive
   folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HhSKNYI2oSPGfY1yIaVIRiTmUKqASDuy?usp=drive_link).

### Suspended players

Players suspended in CPL may not play in Premier League games until their suspension ends.

## CWC qualification

### Who goes to the qualifier

- CPL's CWC qualification is a double-elimination Bo3 tournament (the Qualifier). Only Premier League teams whose
  main community is CPL may take part.
- Teams whose main community is CivFR (Squadrons teams) play in the CivFR CWC qualifying tournament. Teams whose
  main community is CivAus, CivChina, CivCIS, CivJapan, CivUkraine or a similar community play in the CWC Wildcard
  Tournament.

### Taking part

- Each League team that wants to qualify for CWC through CPL must tell the League Director by the deadline the
  Director posts.
- The defending CWC champion has an automatic CWC slot and doesn't play in CPL's qualification.
- If the Premier League Champion's main community is CPL and it isn't the defending CWC champion, it gets CPL's
  first CWC slot and doesn't play in the Qualifier.
- If the team that lost the Premier League final has CPL as its main community and isn't the defending CWC
  champion, it gets CPL's second CWC slot (or the first, if that isn't taken) and doesn't play in the Qualifier.
  Any of CPL's first two slots not filled this way are decided in the Qualifier.
- The eight highest-ranked teams after the post-season may play in the Qualifier if: their main community is CPL;
  they don't already hold the defending champion's slot or a CPL CWC slot; and they are in Division 1 or 2, or in
  Division 3 with a winning regular-season record.
- The League Director may remove Qualifier slots if fewer than eight teams meet these conditions.

### Qualifier structure

- The eight teams are seeded 1 to 8 in their season ranking. Division 1 teams (after advancement and relegation)
  rank above Division 2 teams, which rank above Division 3 teams. A team that wins promotion to Division 1 in the
  Season 6 post-season ranks as a Division 1 team.
- The winner of the winners' bracket takes the top unfilled CPL CWC slot, and doesn't play the winner of the
  losers' bracket.
- The winner and runner-up of the losers' bracket get the second and third unfilled CPL CWC slots.
- If more slots are unfilled, the team knocked out in the round before (losers' bracket round 3) gets the next one.
  If two teams were knocked out in the same round and there is a slot left, they play a Bo3 tiebreak for it.

### Qualifier maps and settings

- The map pool is the regular-season map pool, unless this section is updated.
- Every Qualifier game is a CPL game, not a CWC game. Use the CPL CivDraft preset and the CPL Premier League game
  preset, as for regular-season games.
