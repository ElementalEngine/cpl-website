## Ranked games

- Every CPL game is reported, unless the players agree before the game not to report it. This means every player
  must be a CPL member.
- Cloud games are ranked by default. If you want an unranked game, say so when you advertise it.
- If you are looking for players for a game with players from outside CPL, say up front that the game is
  unranked. CPL players join for the no-quit policy, so they need to know the risk before joining.

## Taking your turn

- You must take your turn within 48 hours, unless you announced a holiday or an emergency beforehand.
- You may ask for more time within those 48 hours, for example: "I'm away for the weekend, I'll take my turn
  by Wednesday."
- If a player hasn't played for 48 hours without notice, first ask them what is going on and whether they will
  play soon. If they reply or play within 24 hours, they stay in the game.
- Moderators decide case by case, after looking into it, whether a player who broke this rule is recorded in the
  PBC problem players channel.

## Substitute votes

- Any player in the game may call a sub vote once 48 hours have passed since the last turn was played. The player
  who hasn't played is left out of the vote.
- The sub vote is a secret vote. Players have 1 day to answer.
- How many players must agree, counted from the last turn played:
  - **3–4 days:** unanimous
  - **4–7 days:** all but 1
  - **More than 7 days with no reply:** no vote needed, the player is subbed out automatically
- If this happens again and again, players can ask a moderator to review the case and allow a sub vote or an
  automatic sub.

## Quitting

Quitting a play-by-cloud game is punished:

1. **1st quit:** you can't join new PBC games, tournaments or leagues for 1 month.
2. **2nd quit:** you can't join new PBC games, tournaments or leagues for 6 months.
3. **3rd quit:** you are banned from joining PBC games.

In PBC, a quit tier drops after 1 year (in real-time games it drops after 90 days).

- Leaving a game without finding a sub yourself is a quit.
- If you leave and the other players find a sub without any help from you, it still counts as a quit.

## Replaying turns

Replaying turns is allowed.

## Unit stacking

All units of the same type (military, civilian, support, naval, religious and so on) that can't normally share a
tile without swapping must be unstacked before you submit your turn. This doesn't apply to Great People.

This replaces the real-time [unit stacking](/rules/exploits/#unit-stacking) rule in cloud games.

## Great People

When you can both recruit and pass a Great Person, you must choose one. Don't shift+enter past the choice: that
locks the Great Person for everyone else for the rest of the round. If you can only recruit and not pass, you
don't have to recruit, because that doesn't lock the Great Person for other players.

## Gold per turn

Trading gold per turn between teammates is not allowed. Trading a lump sum of gold is allowed.

## Congress turn

This rule applies to every game where only public communication is allowed, unless the host said before the game
that it doesn't apply. Games that allow private communication need their own congress rule.

- On a World Congress turn, the host posts the resolutions in the game's channel. If the host doesn't, the next
  player can when their turn comes, and so on until the last player in the slot order. If nobody does, the
  congress happens silently.
- Nobody may discuss the congress without first letting the other players in the game know what the resolutions
  are.
- Once the resolutions are posted, players have 48 hours to share their preferences. The player who posted them
  can't move on until everyone has said they are done, or the 48 hours are up.
- This time doesn't count towards the turn time of the player who posted the resolutions.

## Ending a game

- A Culture or Diplomatic victory is granted when the player posts a screenshot showing the victory condition met.
- A game that finishes, or should finish, with a victory screen only ends when all players still in the game
  agree with the victory, or when the last player in the turn order has played their turn. That player must post a
  screenshot.

This gives every player the chance to win in the same round (a tie for 1st under CPL rules), to show that a
Culture victory wasn't reached that round, and to improve their score in the last round.

## Optional rules for hosts

A host may add these to their game, or let players vote on them, when advertising a new game.

### Nukes

At most one nuke per player per target every X turns. Players may have more than one nuke in stock. X is usually
1, but it is up to the host or a vote.

### Rock Bands

At most X Rock Bands per player at the same time, and the policy that stops Rock Bands entering a territory can't
be used. X is usually 1–2, higher with more players.

Players can reload and change random events so a Rock Band is never lost, and nobody can prove it. This rule is
never put to a vote: a host who uses it must say so when advertising the game, so players who don't like it can
stay out.
