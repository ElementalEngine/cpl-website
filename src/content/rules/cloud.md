## Taking your turn

- You must take your turn within 48 hours.
- You may ask for more time within those 48 hours, for example: "I'm away for the weekend, I'll take my turn
  by Wednesday."

## Quitting

Quitting a play-by-cloud game is punished:

1. **1st quit:** you can't join new PBC games, tournaments or leagues for 1 month.
2. **2nd quit:** you can't join new PBC games, tournaments or leagues for 6 months.
3. **3rd quit:** you are banned from joining PBC games.

In PBC, a quit tier drops after 1 year (in real-time games it drops after 90 days).

## Ending a game

A game that finishes, or should finish, with a victory screen only ends when all players still in the game
agree with the victory, or when the last player in the turn order has played their turn. That player must post
a screenshot.
