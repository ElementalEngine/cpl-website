These rules apply to everyone who uses a CPL service (the Discord server or the Steam group), whether they play
ranked games or not. If you see someone break a rule, or someone abuses you, report it to a staff member straight
away (see [Reporting a rule break](/rules/general/#reporting-a-rule-break)).

## Accounts

### One account

- You may only have one Discord account and one Steam account registered with CPL at a time. The registration bot
  only allows one of each.
- Your Steam account must be linked to Discord at all times. Only the Head Admin or a Vice Head Admin can let a
  player play without it.
- Your Steam account must be public when you register. After that you may make it private, but you must make it
  public again if a moderator asks.
- Epic Games Store players must send a moderator a screenshot of their EGS player screen showing their player name
  and Civ 6.
- Don't use another account to get around a suspension. You will be caught, and the punishment will be much
  harsher.

### Coming back with a new account

- If you come back with a new account, you must tell a moderator who you are, in a private message. Not telling a
  moderator can lead to a suspension of up to 30 days. Doing it again may lead to a permanent ban, which you may
  appeal after 6 months.
- You may leave and rejoin CPL with the same Discord and Steam names. Rejoining under a different name to avoid a
  punishment leads to a suspension.
- Every player may have their stats reset once while they are in CPL, except Deity-level players.

### Usernames and nicknames

- No username or nickname may be offensive or abusive, including about race, religion, sex or political views. You
  will be asked to change an offensive name, and kicked from the server if you don't. A very offensive name may get
  you kicked straight away.
- Your nickname must be close enough to your Steam or EGS name that players know who you are. You may change your
  Steam name when you aren't in a CPL game.
- Your Discord name must start with enough Roman letters to be easy to tag.
- Your name in the game must match, or be close to, your CPL Discord name. Changing your name in a game to another
  player's name breaks the rules.
- Nicknames may not contain emojis, unless you have donated $10 or more to the server. You may add a clan tag.

## Conduct

### Harassment and toxicity

- Be respectful and civil to each other. Some "smack talk" is a healthy part of gaming, but harassment,
  cyberbullying and toxic behaviour are not tolerated.
- Insulting or calling other players names, even in a tense game, may be punished with a suspension.
- Staff decide by vote whether toxic behaviour is a minor, moderate or major infraction (see
  [Punishments](/rules/general/#punishments)).

### The stop rule

- If someone is harassing you, tell them to stop. Be polite, but clear about what you don't accept. Try to calm
  things down before you report someone.
- You can only use the stop rule against offensive communication, not to make someone stop talking in general.
- The stop rule applies to you too. For example, if you ask someone to stop calling you a name, you may not call
  them the same or a similar name.

### Chat

- Chat on Discord and Steam is a privilege, not a right. Spam, toxic behaviour, harassment, or posting anything
  against Discord's or CPL's rules can lead to a suspension, depending on how serious it is.
- Spam may be removed without warning, especially in channels for advertising games or reporting results. Spamming
  a lot can lead to a temporary suspension.
- Don't post the same thing in several channels. Post suggestions and complaints in the suggestions channel, and
  read the how-to-suggest channel first.
- Everything you post, including avatars and animations, must follow the law and the terms of service of Discord,
  Steam and other platforms. Anything inappropriate is removed straight away. The off-topic channels are slightly
  more relaxed.

### Memes about other players

You may not post a meme about another CPL player in CPL channels without their written consent first. A meme here
means an image that makes fun of, and refers to, another CPL player. The player may take back their consent at any
time, and you must then stop posting it. If they want old copies removed, they must find them and link them to the
person who posted them. Screenshot the consent before you post.

### Other leagues

Posts advertising other multiplayer leagues are removed straight away, and the poster is punished. Sites that
aren't multiplayer leagues, such as Apolyton, CivFanatics or WePlayCiv, are fine.

## Banned words

Some words and phrases are banned. They may not be said or typed in any form, including with stars, whatever the
context (for example, singing a song). The list covers slurs and insults about race, ethnicity, nationality,
sexuality, gender identity and disability, words about child abuse, and "kys". Anything about self-harm or threats
of real-life violence is also banned.

Pro-Nazi images or words get zero tolerance.

The full list is pinned in the rules channel of the CPL Discord server.

## Discord Partner Code of Conduct

CPL is a Discord partner, so it must promote positive behaviour. This means some rules are stricter, and moderators
must act quickly when the Code of Conduct is broken. Moderators act straight away on:

- discriminatory jokes and hate speech: attacks on a person or group because of their race, ethnicity, national
  origin, sex, gender, sexual orientation, religion or disability
- moderators or admins verbally abusing, insulting or threatening others, or showing offensive, derogatory or
  explicit content
- supporting or encouraging any of the above
- selling a partnered server or account, which is not allowed

Read the full [Discord Partnership Code of
Conduct](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024871991-Discord-Partnership-Code-of-Conduct).

## Inviting new players

To invite someone to the CPL Discord, type `!invite` in the bot commands channel, or share https://discord.gg/cpl.

## Punishments

Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, a suspension or a permanent ban. See
[Punishments](/rules/general/#punishments) and [Appeals](/rules/general/#appeals).
