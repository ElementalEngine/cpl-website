If you see someone break a rule, or someone abuses you, report it to any moderator straight away.

## Basic rules

1. **No harassment, abuse or toxicity.** Be respectful of others, and stop when asked to stop. See the [Discord
   rules](/rules/discord/).
2. **No quitting.**
   - You may leave a game once you have lost 66% or more of your cities.
   - You may leave a game if the other players vote you irrelevant.
   - Don't sub out of a game unless you have found a substitute for yourself.
   - Being AFK for 5 minutes or more counts as a quit.
3. **Don't lose on purpose.**
   - Don't feed, suicide or delete units.
   - Don't go AFK, especially when you are at war.
4. **Don't use another account to get around a suspension.** You will be caught, and the punishment will be much
   harsher.

Breaking CPL rules can lead to a warning, a suspension or a permanent ban (see [Punishments](#punishments)).

## Substitute players

### When a sub needs a vote

- No vote is needed if the sub is within 2 skill levels of the player they replace.
- **Non-team games:** if the sub is not within 2 ranks of the original player, any player may object before the sub
  joins and hold a secret vote. The vote passes with a simple majority.
- **Team games:** if the sub is more than 2 skill levels away, each team holds its own majority vote to let them
  in. The higher of the player's two ratings (Team or FFA) is used.
- Non-novices may not sub into a novice game without a unanimous vote.

### Joining a game as a sub

- A sub has joined the game once they end their first turn. Until then they may leave freely, so take your time to
  understand the position you are taking over.
- You may not sub into a game you have already played in. You may rejoin an FFA game you were part of if you take
  back your original civ and a majority of the remaining players vote to let you back.
- If no sub is found within 10 minutes of a quit and the game continues with an AI, no sub is allowed later.

### Subbing out

- You may sub out for any reason. You don't need an emergency.
- Finding an accepted sub before you leave is not a quit, but it counts towards your subs for the month.
- You are not released from the game until your sub clicks End Turn. If you leave before that, you may count as a
  quitter.
- After subbing out, wait at least 60 minutes before joining another game. This wait doesn't apply if you lost 66%
  of your cities.

### Sub limit

Up to 3 subs a month is fine. From the 4th sub in the same month, each sub counts as a 3-day suspension.

### When no sub is found

If players can't find and agree on a sub within 10 minutes, they hold a concede vote and a scrap vote, in any
order. If both fail, the game continues with an AI.

### Swapping players in a team game

- You can swap players or civs under the sub rules above.
- The two players must be within 2 skill levels of each other, or the opposing team or teams must allow the swap
  with a majority vote.
- The player being swapped in must already be out of the game: through a successful irrelevancy vote from their
  team, a legal reason to leave, or because they have already died or left. This leaves one player on the team as
  an AI.
- When you report the game, just swap the players or civs in the report. Don't call it a sub: a swap doesn't count
  as a sub.

### Replacing a player before the game starts

- After the game vote ends and before the countdown reaches zero, a player may find a replacement within 2 skill
  levels, as long as the host doesn't object.
- If the replacement is more than 2 skill levels above the player, the lobby must agree unanimously.
- This doesn't count as a sub for the player being replaced, including for game reporting. They are released only
  when the replacement clicks End Turn on turn 1.

## Leaving a game

### When you may leave

- The game starts when the final plus is clicked on the game settings vote. If no settings vote is used, you may
  leave without penalty until you click Ready in the game.
- After that, you may only leave once you have lost 66% of your cities (FFA games only) or after a successful
  [irrelevancy vote](#irrelevancy). Wait until the start of a turn to leave.
- CPL doesn't mind you leaving. It minds your leaving having a big effect on the result of the game.

### Leaving at the end of a game

- The host says when a vote has ended the game. The first player to leave without the host's approval may be
  punished if the vote or the game was not over, because one early leaver often sets off others.
- If the host approved and you can prove it (get it in writing), the host is responsible for any wrong concede,
  scrap or irrelevancy.

## Drop policy

### 10-minute wait

- If someone drops from a game, the host must pause the game for 10 minutes or until the player returns.
- A dropped player has 10 minutes to rejoin without penalty. After that, they may count as a quitter.
- If they don't return within 10 minutes, the remaining players vote to concede or to scrap the game. A majority
  vote can choose to spend up to 10 minutes finding a sub. If the scrap and concede votes fail, and no sub is
  wanted or found, the game continues with an AI.
- You may skip the scrap, concede and sub votes if a vote to continue with an AI passes (simple majority).
- A player who takes longer than 10 minutes may come back if nobody objects. If someone objects, the remaining
  players vote, and a simple majority lets the player back.
- If a player quits and the game is scrapped before their 10 minutes are up, it still counts as a quit, unless the
  player said in Discord that they were coming back.
- If a player says in text or out loud that they are leaving, and disconnects, the host may remove them straight
  away and they get a quit. Leaving the Discord server (not just the voice channel) counts as saying you are
  quitting on purpose.

### 3-drop policy

- Every player, including the host, gets 2 disconnects with no penalty. Firaxis crashes and drops during a relobby
  don't count.
- After a 3rd drop, the other players may vote, by secret vote, to remove the player who dropped:
  - **FFA:** a majority of the players, not counting the player who dropped.
  - **Teamers:** a majority of the opposing team. If the player is not let back in, their teammates have up to 10
    minutes to find a solution (a sub, a concede vote and so on).

## Remaps

### FFA remaps

- Each player may ask for 1 remap, on or before turn 10. It needs a unanimous vote. This applies to remap votes
  without a screenshot.
- At most 2 players may propose remap votes, to avoid lots of separate votes.
- After a remap you may change your civ to another one from your draft.

### Team remaps

- A remap may be granted on or before turn 10 with a simple majority vote.
- Each team gets 1 remap request.
- After a remap you may change your civ to another one from your draft.

### Automatic remaps

- **Pangaea, Highlands, Seven Seas and Lakes:** a remap is granted automatically if you can't settle 3 more cities
  within 5 tiles of the tile your starting settler spawns on. Those tiles must be reachable without crossing water,
  mountains (including going around mountains) or other blocking terrain. The kind of housing on the tile doesn't
  matter: cities off water count.
- **Every other map:** a remap is granted automatically if you can't settle 2 more cities before Shipbuilding is
  researched. There is no distance limit.
- A Firaxis spawn is an automatic remap.
- A starting settler that spawns off the coast and off fresh water is an automatic remap.

### Remaps in every game

- In a game with random civs, players must random again after an agreed remap. Only a simple majority vote can
  keep the same civs.
- Players must stay for the first 2 remaps. If a 3rd remap is needed, they may leave before joining the 3rd
  remap lobby. Loading into the game gives up that right.
- The timer of a timed game starts at the civ draft, so a remap doesn't reset it.
- If the Better Balanced Starts mod isn't used, a remap must be granted if 2 players' starting settlers spawn (not
  settle) less than 9 tiles from each other, less than 6 tiles from a city-state, or less than 5 tiles from the
  edge of the map. A screenshot is needed as proof. With it, no vote is needed.

## Scraps

A scrap vote needs:

- **Turns 1–20:** a two-thirds majority
- **Turns 21–50:** a three-quarters majority
- **Turns 51–70:** all but 1
- **Turn 71 on:** unanimous

Also:

- Leave 10 turns between scrap votes, unless the game changes: for example a player quits, a sub joins, or there is
  a relobby.
- Report every scrap in the scrap reporting channel. If the scrap was because a player left or quit, or because of
  a technical problem, say so in the report.

### Team scraps

- Each team has one vote, decided by a simple majority of the team's players.
- With 2 teams, both teams must agree to a scrap, on every turn.
- With more than 2 teams, the thresholds above apply.

## Irrelevancy

### Irrelevancy in an FFA

- **Turns 1–49:** unanimous.
- **Turn 50 on:** all but 2 of the remaining players, if the player:
  - is one of the bottom 2 players by score, AI included
  - doesn't hold a veto
  - isn't part of an ongoing emergency
- A player who doesn't meet those conditions needs a unanimous vote of the remaining players.

### Irrelevancy for a player on a team

- **Turns 1–49:** unanimous.
- **Turn 50 on:** all but 2 of the remaining team members.

### Irrelevancy for a whole team

For games such as 2v2v2v2:

- **Turns 1–49:** unanimous.
- **Turn 50 on:** all but 2 of the remaining teams, if the team:
  - is one of the bottom 2 teams by score, AI included
  - doesn't hold a veto
  - isn't part of an ongoing emergency
- A team that doesn't meet those conditions needs a unanimous vote of the remaining teams.

### How an irrelevancy vote works

- Only you can start your own irrelevancy vote, by asking for a secret vote. You don't vote on it.
- If the vote fails, wait 10 turns before asking again.
- If the vote passes, leave at the start of the next turn, before making any other move. If you don't leave then,
  or you make a move, the vote no longer counts and you must wait 10 turns to ask again.
- Leaving before the turn has fully rolled over may be punished as unsportsmanlike conduct if it causes technical
  problems.
- A civ voted irrelevant stays an AI for the rest of the game.
- A player who has lost two-thirds of their cities may leave without an irrelevancy vote.

### Civ 7: irrelevancy

- You can't leave the game before losing two-thirds of your cities.
- After you gift settlements, no irrelevancy vote is allowed for the rest of the current age or for 20 turns,
  whichever is longer.

## Voting

### Secret votes

- In FFA games, every concede, irrelevancy and remap vote must use the secret vote bot. The player who starts a
  secret vote must pause the game, and the game stays paused until the host announces the result.
- Every required in-game vote uses a secret vote, except concede votes in Teamers. Votes inside a team in Teamers
  may be held in the game chat.
- Votes to change the game rules, or to let a non-novice play in a novice game, use a secret vote in both FFA and
  Teamers.

### When to vote

- Hold votes in the last 20 seconds of a turn, with the game paused, unless the game is already paused for another
  reason.
- Don't vote during a break. A vote that has been asked for must finish before another vote or a break starts.

### Tied votes

In a tied pre-game vote, the host's vote decides. For example, if 5 players vote for Pangaea and 5 for Seven Seas,
and the host voted for Pangaea, Pangaea wins.

### Leapfrogging

A concede vote that places a player ahead of someone above them (leapfrogging) must be agreed by every player being
passed, except anyone being leapfrogged into first place. The 3rd-place player still counts even if they are being
leapfrogged.

### Relobby votes

A player who wants a relobby for something that doesn't normally need one, such as a misclicked settler, a hotkey
bug or a settler lost to a flood on turn 4, may ask for one. It needs 66% or more of the other players to vote in
favour.

### Pressuring a vote

Unsportsmanlike conduct includes:

- threatening to quit or sub out of a game to force a vote
- trying to find out how players voted in a secret vote
- spamming votes

## Conceding

### How many players must agree to a concede

Games that start in the Ancient Era:

- **Turns 1–80:** unanimous
- **Turns 81–100:** all but 1
- **Turn 101 on:** all but 2

Games that start in a later era (its first turn in brackets):

- **Classical (40):** all but 1 on turns 91–115, all but 2 from turn 116
- **Medieval (71):** all but 1 on turns 106–130, all but 2 from turn 131
- **Renaissance (98):** all but 1 on turns 131–155, all but 2 from turn 156
- **Industrial (128):** all but 1 on turns 156–180, all but 2 from turn 181
- **Modern (158):** all but 1 on turns 176–200, all but 2 from turn 201
- **Atomic (183) and Information (208):** always ironman

Before those turns, a concede vote must be unanimous.

### FFA concedes

- The player in first place can't start a concede vote and doesn't vote on it.
- The player who asks for a concede vote counts as voting in favour and doesn't vote.
- Concede votes must be 10 turns apart, unless something could end the game, such as a quit.
- If you take a city on the turn of a concede vote, you may delay the vote to the next turn. Say in chat that you
  took a city before the vote ends.
- If a concede vote passes, the host must announce that any player who wants to use a veto must DM the host in the
  game chat. If no veto holder does within 2 minutes, the host declares that the concede passed. The host must not
  say which player used a veto.
- Several concede votes may be tried on the same turn if players are negotiating. If a vote fails and the turn
  rolls over, concede votes must be unanimous until 10 turns after the last one, unless something could end the
  game, such as a quit.
- A vote that needs everyone to agree doesn't start the 10-turn wait for the next vote.

### Team concedes

- A team agrees to a concede when more than 50% of its remaining players vote plus.
- With several teams, use the thresholds above and count each team as one plus or minus vote.

### Vetoes

Vetoes only apply to FFA games. A player with a veto must say that they are using it to keep playing during a
concede vote.

- **Score:** the players 2nd and 3rd by score.
- **Science:** the highest science per turn; within 3 techs of the tech leader; launched an Earth Satellite.
- **Culture:** the player with the most foreign tourists, if they have at least 15% of the foreign tourism needed
  to win on turns 81–90, 25% on turns 91–100, or 40% from turn 101.
- **Military:** the player with the highest military score, and any player who holds 3 or more original capitals.
- **Religion:** any player who has converted more than 50% of civs.
- **Diplomacy:** any player with more than 10 points.

## Timed games

- Changing the length of a timed game needs a unanimous vote.
- The draft marks the start of the game: its timestamp is the start time.
- The game ends on the turn after the time runs out.
- Score is the only measure. There are no vetoes and no extra turns for a captured city: the game ends when it is
  scheduled to end.
- The host is responsible for making sure the game ends on time.

## Reporting games

- The winner (or the winning team's captain) must report the game within 2 hours, or before starting another game.
- If the winner doesn't report the game, the 2nd-place player reports it with themselves as the winner and the
  winner in last place.
- If the order can't be agreed, the game is scrapped and every player except the winner gets 5 points.

## Punishments

Infractions other than quits come in four categories, each with its own tiers. Your tier goes down by 1 after the
time shown for its category.

### Minor infractions

A tier drops after 90 days.

- **Tier 1:** warning
- **Tier 2:** 1-day suspension
- **Tier 3:** 2-day suspension
- **Tier 4:** 4-day suspension
- **Tier 5:** 7-day suspension
- **Tier 6:** 14-day suspension
- **Tier 7:** permanent ban from the server

### Moderate infractions

A tier drops after 90 days.

- **Tier 1:** 1-day suspension
- **Tier 2:** 4-day suspension
- **Tier 3:** 7-day suspension
- **Tier 4:** 14-day suspension
- **Tier 5:** 30-day suspension
- **Tier 6:** permanent ban from the server

### Major infractions

A tier drops after 90 days.

- **Tier 1:** 7-day suspension
- **Tier 2:** 14-day suspension
- **Tier 3:** 30-day suspension
- **Tier 4:** permanent ban from the server

### Extreme infractions

A tier drops after 4 years.

- **Tier 1:** 30-day suspension (in some cases, a permanent ban from the server)
- **Tier 2:** permanent ban from the server

### Rule breaks fixed by a relobby

A rule break that a relobby can fix must be fixed that way. These include, but are not limited to:

- Great Person passing
- different war moves
- capturing and keeping more city-states than allowed
- Amani abuse

### Temporary penalties and appeals

- Admins and moderators may give a temporary penalty until other members of the team review the case and post a
  formal review in the suspended players channel.
- Even after a verdict, you may appeal for a second review by the Vice Admins and the Head Admin.
- Staff decide what conduct breaks the rules.

### Self-suspensions

CPL no longer gives or accepts self-suspensions, because they were hard to apply consistently.

## Other rules

### Harming the community

Flame wars, encouraging members to break rules, hacking, cheating, faking game results, pretending to be another
member and publicly recruiting players away from CPL are all banned. You may join and take part in other
communities, but asking players to leave CPL for another community is a serious offence.
